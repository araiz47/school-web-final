Admissions

Join the Eden House family. Start with a quick inquiry, then visit our campus to complete admission.

Send Inquiry
Admissions are completed on campus only. The online form lets us know you're interested and helps us prepare for your visit. No fee or documents are collected online.

How Admission Works

1

Send an Inquiry

Fill in the form below so we can contact you.

2

Visit the Campus

Assessment

4

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I apply online?

The online form is an inquiry only. Admission is completed on campus with the required documents.

What are the fees?

The fee structure is shared at the admissions office.

Is there an entry test?

Younger classes have a friendly interaction, and Class 1 and above have a short test.

When does the new session start?

The information is shared at the admissions office.
